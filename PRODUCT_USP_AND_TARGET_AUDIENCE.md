5. **Aural & Ritual Somatic Induction:**

## 3. Detailed Target Audience Personas

### Persona A: "The Transformational Seeker" (Primary B2C)
* **Demographics:** Age 22–38; Female & Non-Binary skew (65%); Urban professional / Creative.
* **Core Pain Point:** Going through a major life milestone (divorce, trauma recovery, career pivot, spiritual awakening) and wanting a tattoo that honors this shift, but terrified of generic designs that look outdated in 5 years.
* **Emotional Motivation:** Craves personal meaning, protection, and an embodied symbol of their inner sovereignty.
* **Willingness to Pay:** High (₹299–₹1,499 / $9–$29 for personalized natal tattoo reports).

### Persona B: "The Astrology & Energy Enthusiast" (Secondary B2C)
* **Demographics:** Age 18–35; active user of Co-Star, The Pattern, Sanctuary, or Vedic astrology apps.
* **Core Pain Point:** Understands their natal chart deeply, but doesn't have the artistic skills to combine their Moon sign, ruling planet, and Chakra needs into an aesthetically elegant geometric tattoo.
* **Emotional Motivation:** Self-expression, astrological authenticity, aesthetic alignment.
* **Willingness to Pay:** Moderate-to-High for detailed D1 Kundli astrolabes and custom planetary sigils.

### Persona C: "The Conscious Tattoo Artist" (B2B SaaS / Affiliate)
* **Demographics:** Age 26–50; Fine-line, hand-poke, or sacred geometry tattoo studio owner.
* **Core Pain Point:** Spends 4–8 unpaid hours per custom client drafting concepts from vague Pinterest screenshots; clients arrive anxious without clarity on their emotional intention.
* **Emotional Motivation:** Elevating their craft from mechanical skin-printing to a prestigious ritual service; attracting high-ticket conscious clientele.
* **Willingness to Pay:** $29–$99/month for an "AstrInk Studio Edition" to generate client natal briefs, vector stencils, and Muhurta appointment slots.
